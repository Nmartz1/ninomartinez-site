About

Award-Winning
Editor & Producer

Nino Martinez, ACE is an award-winning producer and editor that has worked on hit shows such as Golden Globe winning "Nip/Tuck" on FX, and Emmy winning "Deadliest Catch" on the Discovery Channel.

In 2008 Nino received an Emmy Nomination for "Outstanding Achievement in Editing" for his work on "Sports Science" on Fox Sports.

In 2007 his collaboration and work on the documentary "Swing Low: Hope and Music Helping New Orleans Rise", which chronicled the music scene in New Orleans immediately following Hurricane Katrina, won Best Documentary at the New Orleans Big Easy Film Festival.
