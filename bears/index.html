Fliers that came home

Santa Monica Library October Events
This is the Santa Monica Public Library's October 2026 calendar of youth, family, and teen events across branches. It's for parents looking for free kids'/teens' activities like LEGO Club, Pokémon Club, D&D, and Halloween-themed programs. Various dates Oct 7-24, 2026; see individual events · free
Fortaleciendo a Nuestras Familias
A free 3-session Spanish-language workshop series for parents and caregivers on strengthening family relationships and communication, led by facilitator Aileen Salmaggi. It's for any Roosevelt Elementary parent who speaks Spanish and wants to attend (childcare is available), not specific to any one grade. Thursdays 5:30-6:30pm, Oct 8, 15 & 22 · free · To join: register in person with Ericka or Dolores at the Park Center office in VAP (limited space)
Pledge Week, October 4-9
Pledge Week is the Santa Monica Education Foundation's fundraising drive for academics, arts and athletics in Santa Monica public schools. It is for families and community members who want to donate, and every gift is matched dollar-for-dollar up to $50,000. October 4-9 (Pledge Week is under way now and ends October 9) · To join: Make a gift at smedfoundation.org during Pledge Week if you want it matched. by Fri 10/9
Samohi Orchestras - the Magic of Disney
Samohi Orchestras is putting on a Disney-themed matinee concert with a costume contest, tacos, bake sale, and raffle at Barnum Hall. It's a fun family event open to the community, good for a school-age kid like Izzy. October 10th, concessions open 3:00pm, doors open 3:30pm, show at 4pm · To join: buy tickets at samohiorchestras.org
Santa Monica Rugby Free Youth Clinics
Free youth rugby clinics for ages 6-18 run by Santa Monica Rugby Club at John Adams Middle School. It's for parents interested in a low-cost intro to rugby across several Saturday sessions. October 11, October 15, and October 25, 10:00-11:30 AM · free · To join: Register free at bit.ly/2026rugby
Free Bully-Proof Self-Defense Class
A free one-hour self-defense class on confidence, awareness and practical skills, offered for October Bully Prevention Month by Longevity Youth Foundation. It is for kids ages 7-11, which fits a 3rd grader like Izzy. Saturday, October 17, 2026, 2:30 PM-3:30 PM · free · To join: Reserve a spot at https://www.longevitymac.com/lyf or by scanning the QR code. Questions: 310-394-5188.
