Fliers that came home

Free Family Concert
A free outdoor baby concert by Delirium Musicum followed by a free music class and instrument exploration, hosted by Sol-La Music Academy. It's for babies and young children ages 0-4 (older kids welcome too). September 13, 2026, doors 10AM, concert 10:30AM, music class 11AM · free · To join: RSVP
2026 Boys & Girls Summer Training Series
Santa Monica Volleyball Club's Summer/Fall Training Series offers pre-tryout clinics and skills camps to sharpen volleyball skills before school season or tryouts. It's for boys and girls at various age/skill levels, with a 50% discount for first-time SMMUSD participants. Sunday/Tuesday/Thursday 6:00-8:00PM; Girls 14U Pre-Tryout Camp Sept 14-Oct 3, All Skills Camp 2 Sept 14-Oct 1 · $150-$200 · To join: register
Santa Monica Airport Conversion Project
An update on the Santa Monica Airport Conversion Project's Phase 4, including a framework survey summary and upcoming public board meetings for feedback. It's for community members interested in how the future airport park site is planned. Public meetings: Planning Sept 16, Recreation & Parks Sept 17, Sustainability Sept 21, Airport Sept 28; Phase 4 community event/survey coming this fall
Samohi Legacy Tailgate
This is a tailgate event before a Samohi football game, hosted by the Santa Monica Education Foundation with an optional rooftop viewing area. It's for parents and families wanting a fun evening out, though tickets are limited. September 18, 2026, 5:30 pm Tailgate, 7:00 pm Game · To join: buy tickets at smedfoundation.org/legacy-tailgate
Sustainability Community Meetings
SMMUSD is holding community meetings to gather input on the district's sustainability goals for 2030 and beyond. It's open to students, families, staff, and community members, including the Santa Monica meeting on Sep 23 or the Malibu meeting on Sep 30. Sep 23, 2026, 6:00-7:30 PM (Santa Monica); Sep 30, 2026, 6:00-7:30 PM (Malibu) · free · To join: RSVP
$25,000 Match from Kindo!
This is a fundraising campaign for the Santa Monica Education Foundation where donations are matched dollar-for-dollar up to $25,000, sponsored by Kindo.ai. It's for parents who want to donate and have their contribution doubled. September 10 - September 24, 2026 · To join: donate by Thu 9/24
