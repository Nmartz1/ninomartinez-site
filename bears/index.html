2026 Boys & Girls Summer Training Series
Santa Monica Volleyball Club's Summer/Fall Training Series offers pre-tryout clinics and skills camps to sharpen volleyball skills before school season or tryouts. Sunday/Tuesday/Thursday 6:00-8:00PM; Girls 14U Pre-Tryout Camp Sept 14-Oct 3, All Skills Camp 2 Sept 14-Oct 1 · $150-$200 · To join: register

An update on the Santa Monica Airport Conversion Project's Phase 4, including a framework survey summary and upcoming public board meetings for feedback. Public meetings: Planning Sept 16, Recreation & Parks Sept 17, Sustainability Sept 21, Airport Sept 28; Phase 4 community event/survey coming this fall
