Week of Thursday, October 22 · one dad's list · event day off or minimum day · tap a date to jump

BK update

  • Halloween, Fri 10/30: kids come in costume with clothes underneath (no parents in bathrooms to help change); no weapon props; masks only during the parade. Parade 9:00 AM in the Big Yard, sign-in from 8:40 at the Upper 9th St gate; 3rd-grade families watch from the track perimeter. Class Halloween centers 10:50 AM in the homeroom, parents welcome; to run a center you need current volunteer clearance (email the homeroom teacher), setup help 10:30. School parade details
  • Auction item due Mon 11/2: each class donates one item worth $50 or more (gift card, experience, bottle); submit online, then tell the room reps.
  • Conferences 11/3 to 11/5: one 25-minute slot per family, both teachers, switch halfway; book a slot. No school Tue 11/3; minimum days Wed 11/4 to Fri 11/6, 3rd grade out at 1:15 PM.
  • Movie Night Tue 11/10: doors 5:15, movie 6:00 (GOAT); $15 advance, $18 door, pizza-popcorn-drink combo $7 to $8; order by 11/9.
  • 3rd Grade Family Picnic Lunch Fri 11/13, 11:55 to 12:40, plus Popcorn Friday.
  • Drop-off valet: parent volunteers run the car line at the Lincoln and Upper 9th gates; sign up by 10/30. Class weeks: Wechselberger 3/15 to 3/19, King 4/5 to 4/9.
  • Homework: folders go home Mon to Thu and come back daily; initial the assignment page every day. Math homework this month is writing to explain reasoning in full sentences. Clever apps: Savvas SuccessMaker (math), Lexia (reading), Typing Club; kids work them alone.
  • Share a family tradition: the class invites parents to bring a story, recipe or song (Día de los Muertos or any tradition); email the homeroom teacher to pick a time.
  • Class fund (voluntary; pays for parties, teacher gifts, supplies): Venmo @Kira-Mollis, memo "BK Combo" plus your student's name.
  • Class links: Berger-King WhatsApp · 3rd Grade WhatsApp · Class Padlet

Fliers that came home

Fall Festival at Will Rogers Farm
GROW/WILD invites Will Rogers families to a fall festival picnic at the Will Rogers Regenerative Farm. It is for students and their families in the Santa Monica-Malibu school community. Friday, October 23, 3-5 PM · To join: Bring a blanket and your own refreshments. Snack boxes are available for a suggested donation.
2nd Annual Dog Parade & Costume Contest
This is the free 2nd Annual Howl-O-Ween Dog Parade & Costume Contest, a family event with games, food trucks, face painting, vendors and prizes. It is for families and dog owners; the flier says to bring the family to watch or to register your dog. Saturday, October 24, 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM · free · To join: Register your dog if you want to enter the parade or costume contest. The flier gives no registration method, only ukrocharity.org to learn more.
Free Youth Rugby Clinics in Santa Monica
Santa Monica Rugby Club is offering free youth rugby clinics at John Adams Middle School. They are for kids ages 6-18, so Izzy is eligible. Sunday October 25 and November 15, 2026, 10:00-11:30 AM · free · To join: Register for free at bit.ly/2026rugby
PALS Spooktacular
This is the PALS Spooktacular, a Halloween event at Barker Hangar sponsored by the City of Santa Monica and local sponsors. The flier gives no other details about activities or who it is for. Saturday, October 31, 2026, 5:00 - 8:00 PM
Santa Monica Sports Academy Multi-Sports Camp
Santa Monica Sports Academy is running fall multi-sports camps (flag football, baseball, basketball, speed and agility, dodgeball and more) on school-off days. It is for kids ages 6-14, grouped by age and ability. November 3 and November 11, 9AM-3PM · To join: Register at smgov.net/reserve; Subway lunch can be ordered for delivery
CHANUKAH/HOLIDAY BOUTIQUE
Beth Shir Shalom is holding a Chanukah and holiday boutique with gifts for women, kids, home, Judaica, beauty and jewelry. It also has a raffle and a DJ, and it is open to the general community. November 4, 2026, 3:00-7:00 PM
