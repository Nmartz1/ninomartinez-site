Fliers that came home

2nd Annual Dog Parade & Costume Contest
This is the free 2nd Annual Howl-O-Ween Dog Parade & Costume Contest, a family event with games, food trucks, face painting, vendors and prizes. It is for families and dog owners; the flier says to bring the family to watch or to register your dog. Saturday, October 24, 11:00 AM - 2:00 PM · free · To join: Register your dog if you want to enter the parade or costume contest. The flier gives no registration method, only ukrocharity.org to learn more.
Free Youth Rugby Clinics in Santa Monica
Santa Monica Rugby Club is offering free youth rugby clinics at John Adams Middle School. They are for kids ages 6-18, so Izzy is eligible. Sunday October 25 and November 15, 2026, 10:00-11:30 AM · free · To join: Register for free at bit.ly/2026rugby
PALS Spooktacular
This is the PALS Spooktacular, a Halloween event at Barker Hangar sponsored by the City of Santa Monica and local sponsors. The flier gives no other details about activities or who it is for. Saturday, October 31, 2026, 5:00 - 8:00 PM
Santa Monica Sports Academy Multi-Sports Camp
Santa Monica Sports Academy is running fall multi-sports camps (flag football, baseball, basketball, speed and agility, dodgeball and more) on school-off days. It is for kids ages 6-14, grouped by age and ability. November 3 and November 11, 9AM-3PM · To join: Register at smgov.net/reserve; Subway lunch can be ordered for delivery
SM Baseball Academy Veterans Day Camp
Santa Monica Baseball Academy is holding a Veterans Day baseball camp covering fielding, hitting, throwing, base running, pitching and catching. It is for kids ages 6-14. November 11, 9AM-3PM · To join: Register at smgov.net/reserve; Subway lunch is available (per the Spanish text)
SM Basketball Academy Veterans Day Camp!
Santa Monica Basketball Academy is holding a Veterans Day fall basketball camp where all skill levels are welcome. It is for kids ages 6-14. November 11, 9AM-3PM · To join: Register at www.santamonicabasketballacademy.com (questions: santamonicabasketballacademy@gmail.com); Subway lunch can be ordered for delivery
