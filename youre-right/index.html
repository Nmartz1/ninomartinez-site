Questions

Before you ask.

Is it a real apology?

It's a real person saying it to you, sincerely, about your story. It won't come from the person who hurt you, and we're honest about that. For many people, hearing the words out loud is what matters.

Can she pretend to be my ex?

No. Apologists apologize in their own name and voice. We never impersonate anyone.

What if I don't love my video?

The Real Apology and Closure include complimentary redos. If something went wrong on our side, we refund you.

Is this therapy?

No. If you're carrying something heavy, a counselor is a better first call. In the US you can call or text 988 any time, day or night.
